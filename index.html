Gear

  • Helmet: full face, DOT, ECE or Snell, no cracks. Brake Free and 2 Wheels want it made in the last 5 years. 2 Wheels bans modular (flip up) helmets.
  • Suit: leather, one piece or two piece zipped together. Zip together textile is allowed in novice groups at XCEL and 2 Wheels; never mesh.
  • Gloves: gauntlet style, covering the wrist and overlapping the suit.
  • Boots: over the ankle riding boots. No work or cowboy boots.
  • Back protector: strongly recommended. XCEL requires one above novice. CE Level 2 beats Level 1.

Bike prep

  • Tires: no cracks or dry rot. XCEL wants 75% tread, 2 Wheels 50%. Set pressures yourself; nobody checks.
  • Tape: headlight, signals and every lens (painter's tape). Tape or remove mirrors. Tape wheel weights. Pull the plate.
  • Coolant: regular coolant is fine for novices at XCEL and Brake Free. Water or a glycol free coolant (Engine Ice, Water Wetter) is required in faster XCEL groups.
  • Tech checks: no leaks or loose bolts, kill switch works, throttle snaps shut, chain tension right, kickstand stays up.
  • Safety wire on the drain plug, filter and fill cap is recommended, not required.
  • Cruisers, floorboards and bags aren't allowed at Brake Free.

How the day runs

  1. Gate: sign the gate waiver. At Firebird with XCEL, gates open 7:30 AM Nov to Mar, 6:30 AM Apr to Oct. Don't show up early.
  2. Pit: grab a spot, unload, park the truck away from the pits.
  3. Registration: photo ID, card, rider waiver. You get a wristband or sticker.
  4. Tech: roll the bike and your gear over right away.
  5. Rider meeting: mandatory. Skip it and you don't ride.
  6. Sessions: groups rotate all day. XCEL runs 15 minutes each hour; Brake Free runs six or more 20 minute sessions.

Flags

  • Green: track is open.
  • Yellow: trouble ahead. Roll off, no passing, be ready to change line.
  • Red: session stopped. Hand up, slow to 30 to 40 mph, pit in.
  • Black: pointed at you (if unsure, assume it is). Hand up, get off the line, pit in, see the marshal.
  • Checkered: session over. Hold speed past the flag, then hand up, slow down, pit in.

What to bring

  • Shade. Firebird, Podium and Inde have little or none. Canopy with weights; desert wind picks up fast.
  • Chairs, cooler, lots of water and electrolytes, snacks. No alcohol anywhere.
  • No power at the tracks. Bring a battery or generator for warmers and chargers.
  • Fuel, stands, basic tools, tire gauge, painter's and duct tape, zip ties.
  • ID, health insurance card, cash, sunscreen. And the bike key.
  • May to September: ride the cool morning sessions and drink before you're thirsty.

Before you book

  • Leave your phone in the pit. Brake Free bans phones on track; a crash detection call can send fire and ambulance, billed to you (around $2,500).
  • No helmet cameras at Brake Free or 2 Wheels. Bike mounted cameras need a tether.
  • Medical insurance is required at Brake Free.
  • No refunds: Brake Free is non refundable, non transferable and runs rain or shine. Check each organizer's policy.
  • Pick your real group. At XCEL, signing up too high can end your day with no refund.
  • Chuckwalla charges a $20 gate fee per adult visitor; kids 17 and under get in free with a guardian.
