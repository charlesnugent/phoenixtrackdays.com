First track day?
What to pick, wear and bring

Pick the right day

  • Track day: you ride your own bike in groups by pace. Sign up for the novice or beginner group.
  • School: structured coaching all day. The easiest way to start.
  • Kart track: tight, slow, supermoto and small bike days. Great for learning body position.
  • Skip anything marked Advanced only until you have a few days in.

Gear

  • Leather suit, one piece or two piece zipped together. Some kart track days accept armored textile that zips together.
  • Full face helmet in good shape. Many organizers require Snell or a recent rating.
  • Gauntlet gloves and over the ankle boots. Back protector is often required.

Bike and tech

  • Tires with good tread, brakes and chain in good shape, no leaks.
  • Tape or remove mirrors. Tape lights.
  • Many tracks want coolant drained and replaced with water. Check your organizer's tech sheet.
  • Every bike passes tech inspection before it goes out.

No bike? No problem

  • California Superbike School rents BMW S1000RRs, and its two day camps include riding gear.
  • Bring water, food, shade, sunscreen and a tank of fuel. Desert days get hot from May to September, so ride the morning sessions.

Rules vary by organizer. Read the event page before you go.
